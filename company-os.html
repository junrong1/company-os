Company simulator

You are the CEO. Now run the company.

The floor is your real org chart. Nobody moves on their own — people start working when you assign something, and they stop at the point where only you can decide.

  1. 1

    Assign the work

    Pick a problem from Directives and hand it to a director. He walks to his specialist's desk to hand it off.

  2. 2

    People start moving

    The specialist begins. When they hit something they can't decide, they stop and a "?" appears above them.

  3. 3

    Walk over and decide

    You can clear it from the Decisions tray. Walk over instead and they'll tell you the reason the tray never shows.

The tray is fast and teaches you nothing about why the company works this way. Walking over surfaces tacit knowledge and raises Visibility. That gap is the point of this simulator.
